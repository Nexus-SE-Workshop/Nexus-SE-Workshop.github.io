01 / About

About the workshop

Human Roles in Software Engineering

Humans elicit requirements, build and test systems, share domain knowledge, exercise judgment, and make creative decisions. Large language models and autonomous agents are changing where those responsibilities begin and end.

NEXUS 2027 creates a focused forum for studying that shifting boundary: what people uniquely contribute today, how human-AI and human-human relationships change, and whether some activities inherently require human involvement.

02 / Call for Papers

What can be submitted

Call for Papers

NEXUS 2027 welcomes contributions from researchers, students, and practitioners in software engineering, artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and related areas.

  • Original research and empirical studies on human roles in software engineering.
  • Innovative ideas and well-argued perspectives on how human work may evolve.
  • Industry experience and practical reports from AI-assisted software development.

Unpublished work

Original and unpublished work is welcome. Authors may choose single-blind or double-blind review and, after acceptance, select either the archival track or the journal track.

Previously published work

Previously published work is also welcome. The submission must clearly identify the original publication. It will be reviewed through the same presentation-centered process using single-blind review and, if accepted, may be presented at the workshop but is not eligible for either publication track.

What to submit and where

Submission Requirements

A professional video production is not expected. Reviewers will focus on the research and on clear, complete communication.

RequiredMP4 + PDF

Recorded presentation

Up to 20 minutes. Communicate the problem, core contributions, evidence, and limitations in a self-contained talk.

  • H.264 codec
  • Up to 1080p
  • Maximum 600 MB
OptionalPDF

Extended abstract

Up to 4 pages, excluding references. Required only if you want to retain the archival-track option.

OptionalAny

Supplementary materials

Full manuscript, datasets, code, instruments, or replication packages that support the review.

Single- or double-blind: authors choose. Every submission receives at least three reviews based on relevance, originality, soundness, clarity, and significance.

Where to submitSubmissions will be managed through HotCRP. The workshop-specific link will be posted here before submissions open on 1 December 2026.

After acceptance

What Happens Next

A

Archival track

After acceptance, authors may publish the accepted extended abstract in the FSE 2027 workshop proceedings in the ACM Digital Library.

B

Journal track

Authors may instead present without workshop-proceedings publication and develop a full paper for Software Engineering Nexus. Publication is guaranteed after the journal's subsequent paper review and revision process. Under the journal's currently announced plan, each published paper receives a USD 500 honorarium and the USD 1,600 APC is waived.
